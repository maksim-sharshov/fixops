FixOps is an AI-powered self-healing platform for modern infrastructure.

It monitors containers, logs, metrics, and application code to detect failures and trace them to their root cause.

When a problem occurs, FixOps doesn't just alert the team — it generates a fix, tests it in isolation, and applies it automatically when the solution is safe.

The concept — an immune response, encoded in software

Three reflexes, running constantly

Every biological immune system runs on the same loop: notice, identify, neutralize. FixOps borrows that loop and runs it against your containers, non-stop, without waiting to be asked.

01

Sense

Continuous signals from every running container are compared against a learned baseline, the way antigens are flagged the moment they don't match what's expected.

02

Diagnose

Once something looks wrong, FixOps traces it to its root cause — the log line, the process, the exact failure — instead of just reporting a symptom.

03

Heal

A fix is applied automatically — restart, rollback, or repair — and the container is returned to a healthy state, no human intervention required.
